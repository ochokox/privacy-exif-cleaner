About Privacy Exif Cleaner

Privacy Exif Cleaner is a lightweight browser-based utility designed for people who want to reduce image metadata before sharing photos online.

How It Works

Images are loaded into your browser and re-encoded using standard browser APIs. The application does not need to upload the image to an image-processing server.
