Privacy Policy

Privacy Exif Cleaner is designed to process images locally in your web browser.

Image Processing

Images selected for processing are handled locally by your browser. The application does not contain functionality that uploads your image files to an image-processing server.

Personal Data

Privacy Exif Cleaner does not intentionally collect or store the images, EXIF data, GPS information, or other metadata contained in images processed by the tool.

Cookies and Tracking

The application does not intentionally use advertising or analytics tracking cookies.

Hosting

Like most websites, the hosting infrastructure used to deliver this site may process technical information required to provide web hosting and security services.

This policy specifically concerns the handling of images by Privacy Exif Cleaner.
